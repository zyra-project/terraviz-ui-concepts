Good to know

Real catalog, illustrative layers

The 528 dataset names, categories, themes, keywords, descriptions, coverage dates and thumbnails are taken from the public Science On a Sphere catalog. The map layers drawn on the globe are procedural illustrations generated in your browser — not the actual data.

One engine, ten interfaces

A dependency-free WebGL2 engine morphs a sphere into a flat map (equirectangular, Natural Earth or Mercator), renders day/night, atmosphere and 20+ data looks, and also draws moons and planets. Every concept shares it, plus the catalog store and a playlist that follows you from concept to concept.

Open, accessible, responsive

  • Keyboard first: / focuses search, Esc closes things.
  • Honours reduced-motion and dark/light preferences.
  • Tested from phone to wide desktop.
  • Text contrast measured against WCAG AA (4.5:1) in all 30 themes — apart from text laid directly over imagery or the globe.
  • Thumbnails and web fonts load from the internet; everything else is inside each single HTML file.
